# Community code of conduct

ORD is an open research environment. Anyone can participate, including people who disagree with the project, question its assumptions or prefer not to use AI.

**Expected behaviour:** engage with evidence and arguments rather than personal attacks; welcome first-time contributors; credit others' work; disclose meaningful conflicts; respect privacy; avoid spam, impersonation and promotional financial solicitation. Be explicit about uncertainty and acknowledge corrections.

**Unacceptable behaviour:** harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, doxxing, intimidation, repeated personal attacks, attempts to obtain credentials, scam or investment promotion, or retaliation against someone who makes a good-faith report. A substantial critique of the project's economics is **not** misconduct.

## Reporting and response

For public, nonsensitive conduct concerns, open an issue or contact an active maintainer through public project channels. For threats, sensitive details or misconduct involving a maintainer, use GitHub's platform reporting tools rather than revealing private information in an issue. Once independent community maintainers exist, they should publish an additional confidential multi-person contact channel and independently managed recusal process.

Maintainers should acknowledge reports when they can, protect privacy, seek evidence, avoid conflicts of interest, and apply proportionate measures (warning, moderation, temporary restriction or permanent exclusion). Give respondents a reasonable opportunity to answer allegations where doing so is safe. Document non-sensitive rationale for consequential actions and permit review by other non-conflicted maintainers.

**Limitation:** Until more maintainers and a neutral organisation exist, there is no independent project-level appeals body. Do not claim otherwise; platform mechanisms and the right to fork remain available.

This is an original project policy; it does not claim endorsement from any external code-of-conduct initiative.
