# ORD: questions worth answering before anyone builds a currency

This project explores whether an **open, physically backed, universal economic entitlement** could preserve individual agency in an AI-driven economy. It is research, not a live token or guaranteed payment.

### Is this a cryptocurrency?

Not necessarily. The [open architecture alternatives](research/RFC_0001_RESOURCE_CLAIMS.md) explicitly include an **issuer-neutral distribution mechanism over existing independent resource claims**. No blockchain, token issuer, smart contract or reserve custodian has been selected.

### Why does AI matter if holders can buy anything?

AI and automation motivate the question: if income from human labour becomes less central, what property can an individual hold and freely exchange to participate in productive markets? AI is **not** the proposed controller, issuer, validator or compulsory destination for spending.

### Does Frank have to use AI to benefit?

No. Frank may hold claims, sell them, trade them to Bob for something he values, or request legally enforceable physical redemption according to the asset contract. The ability to redeem and the ability to exchange are distinct; neither is yet implemented by ORD.

### Does universal allocation mean Bob may not acquire more?

No. Allocations are intended as a starting distribution of **already-funded** ownership. Bob can voluntarily purchase additional claims from Frank or anybody else. The proposal does not prohibit stockpiling or require a resource owner to sell.

### Can't the ledger just mint everyone's entitlement?

That would invent liabilities without backing. A dividend pool must own existing claims before transferring them, or legally obtain new assets that are **physically accepted and independently verified before issue**. An automatic issuance schedule is not proof of a funded payment.

### What do the copper numbers mean?

The [research calculator](research/COMMODITY_SCALE.md) shows that even a purely hypothetical 1% contribution of 2025 worldwide mine copper output would amount to about **28.5 grams of copper content per person per year** over 8.3 billion people. There is **no evidence any such metal is available to ORD**, and mined copper is not necessarily deliverable Grade A copper. These figures express a **quantity bound**, not a commodity price, a basic-income amount or a prediction.

### How much would a meaningful recurring resource entitlement cost?

A [public scenario calculator](research/VALUE_AND_FUNDING.md) asks what genuine outside income could purchase in physical copper claims. For illustration only, providing $100 of quoted metal value per year to 8.3 billion people requires about $946 billion of annual settled funding under a specific set of assumed expenses and retained reserves. At an assumed 4% endowment yield, that corresponds arithmetically to about $23.6 trillion of invested capital. No such endowment or yield is real, and acquiring the necessary copper at a fixed spot price is implausible. The project does not propose a dollar-pegged token.

### Would an immutable ledger prevent corruption?

It makes an independently witnessed transaction history more difficult to rewrite secretly. It does **not** establish that physical metal exists or has unencumbered legal title. In fact, the [dual-book audit illustration](examples/compare_books.py) shows an internally consistent journal with *ten* units of copper liabilities but only *eight* units of separately declared inventory. The ledger is mathematically consistent; the declared collateral is insufficient. Even a matched declaration is not yet a verified audit.

### Can anyone redeem fractions of a wholesale bar?

Not established. Wholesale physical markets have delivery units such as 25-tonne LME copper lots and large LBMA Good Delivery bars. Smaller private holdings can be transferred or aggregated, but **small-holder delivery rights, handling costs, title and insolvency protection must be demonstrated separately**. See [real delivery evidence](research/REDEMPTION_EVIDENCE.md).

### Why only gold, silver and copper, and how much would the three provide?

They are **initial research candidates**, not a selected fixed basket or exclusive definition of scarcity. The [three-metal analysis](research/THREE_METAL_COMPARISON.md) uses separately sourced 2025 mine output for all three and deliberately hypothetical price quotations. An invented contribution of 1% of each metal's entire annual global mine flow would provide about $1.05 in market-reference value per person across 8.3 billion people, before costs. **No such contribution exists**, and mined flow is not the same as available metal or existing aboveground holdings. Other resources, backing structures and even an issuer-neutral distribution method remain open proposals.

### Would I legally own actual metal or an IOU from a company?

That is **not yet decided**, and the distinction matters. The [LBMA's official market account guide](https://www.lbma.org.uk/publications/the-otc-guide/precious-metal-accounts) differentiates allocated holdings linked to specific identified bars from unallocated balances owed by a clearing institution. The [LME's electronic warrant system](https://www.lme.com/en/sustainability-and-physical-markets/warehousing/lme-warrants) is another example of actual metal title documentation. Our [legal-title research note](research/TITLE_AND_CUSTODY.md) explains what must be established for an ORD holder. None of these precedents presently issues, backs or guarantees any ORD claims.

### Why not just use Paxos, Kinesis, GoodDollar or Circles?

Because **each solves a different part**, and some would change ORD's promise. [Paxos PAXG](https://www.paxos.com/terms-and-conditions/pax-gold-terms-conditions) describes fractional ownership of allocated bars, but requires 430 PAXG plus fees for direct bar delivery through verified customers and retains specified freeze powers. [Kinesis](https://support.kinesis.money/hc/en-gb/articles/38687923843741-Redeeming-Gold-and-Silver-in-App) documents 100 KAU or 200 KAG redemption increments, with handling/shipping costs. [GoodCollective](https://github.com/GoodDollar/GoodCollective) has genuinely funded, MIT-licensed UBI pool logic, but distributes G$, **not legal metal title**. [Circles v2](https://docs.aboutcircles.com/overview/how-it-works/personal-currency) issues ongoing personal currencies rather than metal-backed claims. The [source-backed review](research/EXISTING_PROJECTS_DUE_DILIGENCE.md) and [interoperability questions](research/INTEROPERABILITY_SPEC.md) separate what ORD might reuse from what none of these systems has proven.

### Is the idea proven novel or economically viable?

No. [Prior art](PRIOR_ART.md) documents overlapping work in universal income, AI windfalls, resource dividends, physical commodity claims and open compute. The combination warrants research but is neither demonstrably unprecedented nor economically validated.

### Who owns this project, and can others challenge it?

The original GitHub repository remains owned by its founder, but [governance](GOVERNANCE.md) allows public contributions, criticism and maintainership. No endorsement of the founding hypothesis is required. The [community discussion](https://github.com/Christopher-Charman/open-resource-dividend/discussions/5) welcomes independent maintainers. A genuinely independent maintainer has not yet been verified.

### Is anyone being asked for money or private information?

No. There is no issued ORD asset, reserve, allocation, fundraise, issuer, vault, wallet, investment offer or guaranteed return. Do not send money, identity documents, private keys or personal records to anyone claiming to represent ORD. This project seeks public **criticism, evidence and open-source research**, not deposits.
