# External ecosystem diligence: what ORD can reuse instead of reinventing

**Source review: 31 October 2026 · Public primary documentation and source repositories · Research only.**

This is an examination of existing organisations and implementations, **not a partnership list, endorsement, licence to copy code, reserve attestation, investment recommendation or confirmation of technical interoperability**. An organisation's statements about its own reserves or user numbers are categorised as **issuer claims** unless actual third-party evidence is inspected and its scope established. Public source code does not establish legal title to metals.

## Executive finding

**ORD's proposed combination is already represented by identifiable pieces of existing systems, but not as a completed whole.** The strongest near-term research composition is to examine **funded, open distribution mechanisms** (GoodCollective/GoodDollar) separately from **existing allocated physical metal claims** (Paxos PAXG / Kinesis KAU/KAG), while studying **AI-generated wealth funding** (Windfall Trust) and **competing compute markets** (Akash). Circles v2 contributes a different, socially grounded identity/trust and transfer model, but **its continually minted personal currency does not satisfy the proposed metal backing invariant**.

The critical missing interoperability is *not* a Solidity ABI: it is an enforceable legal transfer of actual existing ownership to **each individual recipient**, independent complete liability evidence, cost-effective physical exit, global eligibility and **legitimate committed funding**. No reviewed project currently establishes all of these for ORD.

## 1. Physical title, collateral, custody and exit

| Existing system | Verified from provider's public documentation | Important constraints and independence questions | Research verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Paxos PAX Gold (PAXG)** | [PAXG terms, last modified 12 Dec 2025](https://www.paxos.com/terms-and-conditions/pax-gold-terms-conditions): each token corresponds to one fine troy ounce of allocated London Good Delivery gold; token holders are described as beneficial fractional owners of segregated bars. Terms 5.2–5.3 describe serial/purity/weight lookups and possible bar reallocation. [Paxos attestation directory](https://www.paxos.com/paxg-transparency) reports monthly reports, issued by KPMG for reports after Feb 2025. | **Only verified customers** may redeem through Paxos under terms 4.11. Section 11.4 requires a **minimum 430 PAXG plus fees** to receive a physical Good Delivery bar; section 14 permits freezes/upgrades, and section 15 contemplates possible future storage fees and specified account charges. A fractional ownership statement is not an independent jurisdiction-specific legal opinion. The public attestation page must be checked for scope/date and does not itself certify universal small-holder redemption. [Terms](https://www.paxos.com/terms-and-conditions/pax-gold-terms-conditions) | **Strong allocated-bar/title design reference; NOT a universal retail-exit-ready ORD asset.** Candidate for *legal and custody diligence only*, not automatic admission or integration. |
| **Kinesis KAU/KAG** | [Kinesis definitions](https://support.kinesis.money/hc/en-gb/articles/12356541049117-What-are-KAU-and-KAG): **1 KAU = 1 gram of gold**, **1 KAG = 1 troy ounce of silver**, with provider-claimed allocated ownership. [Kinesis audit index](https://kinesis.money/audits/) reports twice-yearly third-party bullion inspection and lists an **April 2026** report. [Provider's redemption steps](https://support.kinesis.money/hc/en-gb/articles/38687923843741-Redeeming-Gold-and-Silver-in-App) specify **100 KAU or 200 KAG increments** and a stated **0.45% of order value + US$100 administration + variable packaging/postage** (verify actual terms before relying on a quote). | These are much larger than many imagined annual per-person entitlements. Provider operations, on-boarding, actual title terms, geographical eligibility, third-party audit scope and practical off-provider exits remain to be assessed. A user may trade fractions, but tradeability alone is NOT entitlement to physical delivery in those fractions. **KMS Labs' wrapped ERC-20 representations** warn that underlying KAU/KAG vault audits must not be misrepresented as independent proof of a *separate token wrapper's* liabilities or reserves. [KMS Labs disclosure](https://kmslabs.money/) | **Strong gold/silver storage and actual fee/minimum precedent; NOT presently proven to support cost-effective global individual physical exits.** Separate original currencies from wrapped forms. |

### Specific safety/contractual finding

Paxos' legal terms explicitly preserve substantial issuer controls over access, conversion and freezing. Kinesis publishes significant fixed administration and delivery frictions. **A third-party gold or silver asset should never be described as "permissionless real-metal redemption" merely because its token can be transferred peer-to-peer.** Actual physical delivery still depends on an enforceable agreement and a capable custodian.

Attestation cadence (monthly or semiannual) differs from continuous independent reserve assurance. No completed ORD due diligence has yet established the completeness of external liabilities, beneficial property rights in all recipient jurisdictions, insurance recovery, double-pledging controls or cross-system settlement finality.

## 5. Architecture implications

1. **Do not build a new token issuer merely because an open funded-pool model exists.** Instead identify whether an independently evidenced external legal property claim can be transferred from a funded entity to an actual recipient through an authorised route.
2. **Do not equate verified token balance with physical ownership.** Require issuer-specific rights/beneficiary structure, physical custody, legal transfer and complete liabilities, not just on-chain supply.
3. **Model small-holder exit explicitly.** Published Kinesis and Paxos physical-delivery sizes are prohibitive for many tiny allocations; research voluntary aggregation, competing buyers and actual legal delivery/cost terms, while preserving the recipient's freedom to decline to trade.
4. **Do not conflate GoodDollar's reserve model with bullion title, or Circles' social currency with metal reserves.** Use their tested research and design ideas, not their monetary ontology.
5. **Do not use one audit to attest a separately wrapped token.** Each layer's receipts/claims and total liabilities need independent reconciliation; no double counting the same collateral.
6. **Leave Akash optional.** Frank must not have to consume compute; Bob can pick any supplier.
7. **Respect source licences.** GoodCollective and GoodProtocol README/licence files were inspected; MIT applies to those source repositories. Current Circles v2 is AGPL-3.0. **ORD includes no transplanted third-party code** as part of this evidence review.

## 6. Concrete next implementation research

**Research priority: understand GoodCollective's funded distribution pattern and test it against rights-bearing asset classes, before considering any integration.** The [ORD issuer-neutral reference](RFC_0002_ISSUER_NEUTRAL_DISTRIBUTION.md) already transfers synthetic existing claims. A credible next *external* step requires lawful provider documentation of (1) asset-holder legal ownership, (2) third-party transfer authorisation, (3) individual registration/eligibility constraints, (4) custodian API or transfer standards, (5) independent liabilities-and-reserves evidence, (6) real redemption limits/costs, and (7) security/legal approvals for handling actual value. No asset/recipient/partner has been admitted.

Potential independently answerable questions for provider documentation or specialist dialogue, **not a request to buy anything**:

- **Paxos/Kinesis:** Is there a legally recognised assignment of a *fractional* allocated-title claim from an independent endowment to an unrelated global human recipient? Does a recipient need an individually KYC'd account? Can they transfer to another licensed custodian without a mandatory issuer repurchase? What costs, jurisdiction exclusions and minimum delivery apply? What can an independent reviewer inspect to reconcile all direct, wrapped and custodied liabilities?
- **GoodCollective:** Can a pool be funded with, and distribute, **third-party legally titled non-fungible or multi-issuer commodity rights** without converting them to G$ or introducing a privileged custodian? Which deployment modules are actually active and which are experimental?
- **Windfall Trust researchers:** Which legally committed future revenue rights, if any, could support a neutral fund whose participants choose to hold physical assets, without an AI company controlling the payment rail?
- **Circles:** Which social-trust/uniqueness lessons are adaptable without importing continuously minted CRC as reserve backing?
- **Akash:** How could independent compute merchants quote/accept optional resource claims through open exchange, without a special ORD or Akash redemption rule?

**Screening result as at this review:** `PAXG = diligence candidate; KAU/KAG = diligence candidate; GoodCollective = design donor; GoodDollar/Circles = distribution and identity references; Windfall Trust = income-funding reference; Akash = optional seller venue`. **Zero external commodity systems are accepted as ORD-ready financial adapters.** No implied affiliation or consent.
