# Open questions and critical assumptions
**Status:** unanswered research items. These are not engineering tasks and no token issuance is authorised.

## Essential economics
- **Source of the dividend:** Which real and legally owned resource inventory or revenue funds the universal distribution? Who contributes it, on what legitimate basis, and how often?
- **Conservation:** Can a recurring entitlement be distributed without creating more enforceable claims than unencumbered physical assets?
- **Distribution scale:** What fraction of actual resources could be made available globally without distorting physical commodity markets?
- **Volatility:** Why gold, silver and copper? Would three independently redeemable classes avoid the hidden exchange-rate and basket-substitution assumptions of a single fungible unit?
- **Reciprocal market participation:** What fraction of individuals would have useful and lawful opportunities to exchange with other people and with providers? Is that enough for genuine universal economic utility?

## Physical and legal rights
- Is a holder a legal co-owner of identified metal, or an unsecured creditor with a delivery promise?
- How are grade, purity, weight, warehouse location and cross-border delivery specified?
- What if the vault is insolvent, the material is missing, or the reserve was pledged twice?
- Can copper be divided or delivered economically at small retail scales?
- Who pays for storage, insurance, audits, shipping and taxes?

## Protocol and privacy
- Can independent parties verify **liabilities as well as reserves** without exposing individuals' holdings?
- What identity/eligibility method prevents duplicate claims without centralising surveillance?
- How are governance amendments constrained? What can be paused during theft or a custody dispute?
- Can an attacker obtain overlapping claims through wrapped assets, bridges, conflicting ledgers or forged warehouse receipts?

## AI and markets
- Why should a metal-backed entitlement provide sufficient access to scarce AI capacity?
- How do sellers quote compute across non-equivalent model tokens and differing quality/latency?
- Does the open market actually resist infrastructure concentration, or are physical bottlenecks decisive?

## Research acceptance threshold
Before any proof-of-concept *with real value*: fund provenance, enforceable title, reserve and liabilities audit, market and cost modelling, privacy/security review, and independent legal analysis must be published and critically examined. An immutable event log alone is insufficient.
